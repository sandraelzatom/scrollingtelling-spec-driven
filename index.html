1950s – 1960s

The Genesis

1950: Alan Turing proposes the Turing Test.

1956: John McCarthy coins "Artificial Intelligence."

1966: ELIZA, the first chatterbot, is created.

1970s

The AI Winter

Computers are too slow and data is too scarce. Funding is cut and research stalls.

1980s

Expert Systems

AI simulates experts in medicine and chemistry. Backpropagation allows neural networks to learn.

1990s – 2000s

Symbolic Triumph

1997: Deep Blue defeats Garry Kasparov.

2000s: The Internet provides "Big Data" for training algorithms.

2010s

The Deep Learning Boom

2012: AlexNet revolutionizes image recognition.

2016: AlphaGo defeats world champion Lee Sedol.

2020s – Present

Generative Era

The rise of LLMs and Transformer architectures. AI moves from analyzing to creating original content.
