1950s – 1960s: The First Wave

The Genesis

1950: Alan Turing publishes "Computing Machinery and Intelligence," proposing the Turing Test.

1956: The term "Artificial Intelligence" is coined at Dartmouth by John McCarthy.

1966: MIT’s ELIZA becomes the first chatterbot.

1970s: The Great Stagnation

The First AI Winter

Programs struggled with "common sense" and real-world complexity.

Government funding was slashed as computers were too slow and data was too scarce.

1980s: Expert Systems

The Resurgence

AI simulates human decision-making in specific domains like medicine.

Backpropagation is developed, allowing multi-layer neural networks to learn.

1990s – 2000s: Symbolic Triumph

Deep Blue & Big Data

1997: IBM’s Deep Blue defeats Garry Kasparov in Chess.

2000s: The Internet creates "Big Data," the fuel for modern Machine Learning.

2010s: The Turning Point

The Deep Learning Boom

2012: AlexNet achieves a massive leap in ImageNet accuracy.

2016: AlphaGo defeats world champion Lee Sedol.

2020s – Present

Generative & Agentic AI

The rise of Transformer architectures and LLMs.

Focus shifts to Agentic AI and multimodal systems (Text, Audio, Visual).
